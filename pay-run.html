01 · Six steps

Six checksbefore the run.

Run payrollSep 2026 · sample

2 of 6 steps looked at · not drawn yet

Payroll draws only from a closed attendance month. Each step shows what is waiting, and records who marked it as looked at.

Step 1

Looked at

Leave, attendance and payable units

Pending leave and corrections decided here. Loss of pay comes from the closed month; LOP reversal puts a day back, with a reason.

Step 2

Looked at

New joinees and exits

Everyone joining this month or on notice, with units worked and salary. If the full and final pays the last month, the run leaves them out.

Step 3

Bonus, salary revisions and overtime

Bonus lines, revisions in the month or back-dated with arrears worked out month by month, and overtime as approved.

Step 4

Reimbursements, adhoc payments, deductions

Approved claims pulled in with one button and marked paid. Or import your own Excel sheet, with a preview before saving.

Step 5

Salaries on hold and arrears

Hold or void a salary, hold part of the payout, or mark it already paid: each with a comment.

Step 6

Override PT, ESI, TDS and LWF

Your figure for one person for one month, beside the computed one, with a note on why.

02 · Pay to the day

Paid for the daysthey were on the roll.

  • Joiners and leavers

    Paid from the joining day, or to the last working day. Nobody types a day count.

  • Revisions, from their date

    Each day at the gross in force that day, so a mid-month revision pays part of the month at each rate.

  • Back-dated? Arrears in one click

    Worked out month by month against what was drawn, then added as one arrears line.

03 · Review and lock

Read it through,then lock it.

  • Everyone, before anyone is paid

    People drawn, gross, deductions and net, then each person’s gross, LOP, TDS and net pay.

  • Lock the month

    A month never drawn can’t be locked. Steps not looked at are named first; once locked, the steps take no more edits.

  • Unlocking needs a reason

    Written down when you unlock, and kept on the audit record with who did it and when.
