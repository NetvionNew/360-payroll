02 · PF and ESI

PF and ESI,line by line.

  • PF. 12% of basic, shown as “PF (12% of basic)”. Someone who opted out with Form 11 gets no PF line.

  • ESI. 0.75% of gross, only while the monthly gross is ₹21,000 or less. Above that, no ESI line.

  • The employer’s share. Employer PF and ESI are worked out for the Cost to company report; the PF register lists UAN, PF wages and contributions.

03 · Income tax (TDS)

Old or New regime,spread over the year.

  • Both regimes. Tax for the year under the regime on each record, Old or New, with the declarations HR keeps there.

  • The months left. What is still due, after TDS already taken this year (a previous employer’s too), is spread over the remaining months.

  • Working kept. The calculation is saved on each payslip, and the payroll screen shows who would pay less on the other regime.
