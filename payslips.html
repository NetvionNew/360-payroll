01 · Payslip Studio

Payslips for the run,and for everything else.

  • Templates

    For payslips issued from the Studio: pick the fields and blocks, from PAN, UAN and bank to leave balance and a signature.

  • Off-cycle payslips

    For someone paid outside the monthly run. Once issued, it is frozen and can’t be deleted.

  • Contractor payment advices

    A payment advice under s.194J, kept apart from salary payslips under s.192.

03 · Loans & claims

Lent once,recovered on the payslip.

  • Salary advances and staff loans

    No interest, flat or reducing balance, within limits you set: how much, how long, and an instalment cap.

  • EMIs on the payslip

    One button pulls due and overdue EMIs into the run and marks them recovered. An instalment can be skipped or waived.

  • Claims never paid twice

    Approved claims join the run with one button and are marked with the month that paid them.

04 · Full and final

Settled from the record,when someone leaves.

  1. Drafted
  2. Agreed
  3. Paid
  • Drafted for you: final salary from attendance, leave encashment, gratuity, and recoveries such as notice shortfall.
  • Agreed, then paid: with a note of what was agreed and with whom, and a statement to print.
  • No double pay: if the settlement pays the last month, the pay run leaves that person out.
