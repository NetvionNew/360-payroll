02 · Questions

Straight answersabout payroll.

Which statutory deductions does it work out?

PF at 12% of basic (left off for anyone who opted out on Form 11), ESI at 0.75% while gross is ₹21,000 or less, professional tax, TDS and the Labour Welfare Fund, on every payslip. Employer PF and ESI are worked out for the reports.

Does it file PF and ESI returns?

No. It works out the contributions and gives you the PF, ESI, PT and TDS registers, and a Statutory tab in the finance workbook with UAN, PF and ESI wages. You file and pay on the government portals; it does not create the ECR file or challans.

How is professional tax worked out?

A flat ₹200 a month by default. Where a person’s state slab differs, HR sets their figure for the month on the Override screen, with a note of why.

Old or New tax regime?

Both. Each employee is on the New regime unless HR sets Old, with their declarations, on the employee record. TDS is the year’s tax less what is already deducted, spread over the months left, and a hint shows who would pay less on the other regime.

Can we set our own salary structure?

The pay run uses a fixed split: Basic 50% of the monthly gross, HRA 20% and Special allowance the rest. Bonus, overtime, arrears and other one-off payments are added as their own lines.

Can a locked month be changed?

A locked month refuses edits to its steps. To change it, unlock the month with a written reason, which goes in the audit log, make the change and lock it again.

Does it pay salaries into bank accounts?

It does not send money. The finance workbook has a Bank advice tab with each person’s bank, account number, IFSC and amount, in Excel, to take to your bank. Anyone missing bank details is listed separately.

Can employees see payslips on their phones?

Employees see their own payslips in the 360 HRMS web portal and can print them or save them as a PDF. Payslips are also emailed to their official address. The employee phone app covers expenses, loans and overtime, not payslips.
