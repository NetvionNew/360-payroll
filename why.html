02 · The challenge

Six month-endpayroll pains.

And the part of 360 Payroll that takes each one off the desk.

Leave still open

Payroll draws only from a closed attendance month; pending leave is settled in step one.

Mid-month joiners

Pay is worked out to the day, from the joining date or to the last working day.

Back-dated increments

Arrears are worked out month by month and added in one click.

Claims paid twice

Reimbursements, overtime and loan EMIs flow in once and are marked paid.

Statutory by hand

PF, ESI, PT, TDS and LWF on every payslip, with a per-person override.

Edits after the run
